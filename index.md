## Project Showcase

### EDA of Five Real-World Datasets
**Research Question:** How can EDA reveal meaningful patterns across weather, cars, police stops, COVID-19, and housing prices?

**Visualization & Findings:**

![Visualization 1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/clin689/data-science-project/main/images/1.png)

*The plot shows insights from the dataset.*

Key findings include:
- Male search rate during police stops (4.68%) is double that of females (2.24%).
- US had the highest COVID-19 confirmed cases (1,039,909).
- England's average house price tripled from 1995 to 2020.
